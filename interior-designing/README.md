# Interior Designing Cheatsheet

Comprehensive quick-reference guide for interior designers — measurements, standards, color guidelines, material specifications, lighting requirements, space-planning rules, and quick formulas. Use this during design projects, client meetings, and specification writing.

---

## 📐 Space Planning Standards & Measurements

### Minimum Clearances (Inches)

| Clearance | Minimum | Preferred | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hallway Width | 36" | 42-48" | Main circulation paths |
| Doorway Width | 32" | 36" | Clear opening for passage |
| Seating Area from Table | 18" | 24-30" | Allows comfortable movement |
| Bedside Clearance | 30" | 36-48" | Both sides of bed |
| Kitchen Work Aisle | 42" | 48" | Single-cook kitchen |
| Bathroom Counter Clearance | 21" | 30" | From vanity to opposite wall |
| Sofa to Coffee Table | 14-18" | 18-24" | Comfortable reach |

### Room Size Standards (Square Feet)

**Living Spaces**
- Living Room: 150-300 sq ft
- Bedroom (Master): 160-250 sq ft
- Bedroom (Secondary): 100-150 sq ft
- Dining Room: 120-200 sq ft

**Work Spaces**
- Office (Private): 100-150 sq ft
- Cubicle: 50-100 sq ft
- Conference Room: 200-400 sq ft
- Reception: 150-300 sq ft

**Functional Spaces**
- Kitchen: 70-150 sq ft
- Bathroom: 35-50 sq ft
- Laundry: 35-50 sq ft
- Closet: 25-50 sq ft

### Work Triangle & Kitchen Layout

Total distance between sink, stove, and refrigerator should be **12-26 feet** (ideal: 15-20 feet).

| Layout | Work Triangle | Counter Depth | Other |
|---|---|---|---|
| Galley Kitchen | 12-16 feet | 24" | Aisle width: 42-48" |
| L-Shaped Kitchen | 8-15 feet (width) | 24" | Corner angle: 90° |

### Color Temperature Guidelines

| Temperature | Name | Mood | Best Used In |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2700K | Warm White | Cozy, intimate | Residential, hospitality |
| 3000K | Warm White | Comfortable, inviting | Bathrooms, kitchens |
| 4000K | Neutral White | Neutral, alert | Offices, retail |
| 5000K+ | Cool White | Energetic, clinical | Task areas, retail |

### Quick Lighting Formula

**Number of Fixtures Needed = Room Area (sq ft) ÷ Spacing (feet)**

Example: 200 sq ft room ÷ 8 ft spacing = 25 fixtures

## 🎨 Color Theory Quick Reference

### 60-30-10 Design Rule

- **60% Dominant color** — walls, large furniture
- **30% Secondary color** — smaller furniture, accents
- **10% Accent color** — accessories, art, details

### Color Psychology in Interior Design

| Color | Psychology | Best Used | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red | Energy, passion, warmth | Dining areas, accent walls | Large areas (overwhelming) |
| Blue | Calm, trust, focus | Bedrooms, offices | Cold if pure/saturated |
| Green | Balance, growth, nature | All spaces, therapeutic | Yellowish green (sickly) |
| Yellow | Optimism, warmth, joy | Kitchens, accent walls | Bright (eye strain) |
| Neutral | Calm, flexible, classic | Base colors, flexible | Can be dull without accents |

## ♿ Accessibility Standards (ADA)

**Essential Accessibility Measurements**

- **Doorway:** 32" minimum clear opening (36" preferred)
- **Hallway:** 36" minimum width (48" for two-way traffic)
- **Ramp slope:** 1:12 ratio (1" rise per 12" run, max 5% grade)
- **Counter height:** 34-36" standard, 36" accessible
- **Toilet seat height:** 17-19"
- **Grab bars:** 1.25-1.5" diameter, 33-36" height
- **Turning radius:** 60" diameter (36" minimum)
- **Light switches:** 15-48" height

## ✨ Design Principles Quick Reference

- **Balance** — Equal visual weight (symmetrical/asymmetrical)
- **Emphasis** — Focal point drawing attention
- **Movement** — Guiding the eye through space
- **Proportion** — Relationship of elements to each other
- **Rhythm** — Repetition creating visual pattern
- **Unity** — Cohesive, coordinated appearance

## 📏 Common Design Formulas

- **Golden Ratio (1:1.618)** — Apply to furniture scale, room proportions, artwork placement
- **Rule of Thirds** — Divide space horizontally and vertically into thirds; place focal points at intersection points
- **Furniture-to-Ceiling Height Ratio** — Furniture height should be 0.3-0.5 of total room height
  - For 8' ceiling: furniture height (seated level) 30-36"
  - For 10' ceiling: furniture height 36-42"

**Rug Size Calculation**
- Residential living room: 8' x 10' or 9' x 12'
- Dining room: 10' x 14' (accommodates chair pull-out)

## ⚠️ Common Design Mistakes to Avoid

- Insufficient clearances causing poor circulation
- Oversized furniture overwhelming small spaces
- Poor lighting creating dark, oppressive spaces
- Too many colors creating visual chaos
- Inadequate storage causing clutter
- Uncomfortable seating heights and depths
- Ignoring views and natural light
- Designs that ignore user needs and workflows
- Materials selected for appearance only (no durability)
- Lack of focal points and visual interest

## 🌳 Quick Design Decision Trees

**Choosing Paint Color**
1. **Start with:** Lighting analysis (north, south, east, west exposure)
2. **Consider:** Desired mood and purpose of space
3. **Test:** Large samples (12"x12"+) for 3-5 days
4. **Verify:** Color appearance at different times of day
5. **Confirm:** How it coordinates with furnishings

**Selecting Flooring**
1. **Analyze:** Traffic level and use intensity
2. **Consider:** Maintenance requirements and durability
3. **Evaluate:** Budget and lifecycle costs
4. **Assess:** Aesthetic integration with overall design
5. **Verify:** Samples in actual space lighting

## 💬 Quick Client Communication Tips

- Establish clear project scope and deliverables upfront
- Confirm budget and timeline expectations
- Present designs with visuals (renderings, boards, samples)
- Document all decisions in writing
- Schedule regular check-in meetings
- Explain design rationale and recommendations
- Manage expectations realistically
- Create change order procedures for scope adjustments
- Provide material and finish options with cost comparisons
- Maintain contingency in budget and timeline
